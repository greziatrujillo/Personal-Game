# Up-the-Cave
A group of friends traveling together in representation of the Sonder Guild are scaling up a mountain to get through to the next town. In an unexpected turn of events, they had to take a detour out of their established path. As they wander through this strange, new path, a few of the guild members happen to fall into a cave that spans meters upon meters below ground. They must work together to climp upward in hopes of reaching an exit near the top, reuniting with the rest of their guild members. 

Luckily, each member has their own set of abilities and skills which will help them get through different sections of the cave.

### Other Information
This game has primarily been developed using [Unity version 6000.3](https://unity.com/getting-started-with-unity) and free assets taken from Unity and different sources that have specified free use for the public.
